## 💼 A Career With a Few Interesting Turns

### Digital Systems & International Conferences

I've managed the operational and technical infrastructure behind international academic conferences, working with researchers, universities and organisations around the world.

My work has included:

- Developing and managing conference websites
- Configuring abstract submission and peer-review systems
- Managing registration and payment platforms
- Administering Linux servers and web infrastructure
- Managing academic publishing systems and digital archives
- Developing workflows and automations between platforms
- Supporting international teams and improving operational processes

One particularly interesting project involved migrating approximately 20 years of academic publishing records into a searchable digital library.

### Salesforce & Business Operations

I'm a **Salesforce Certified Administrator**, with experience in CRM configuration, user management, reports, dashboards and business processes.

Before moving further into technology, I worked in business and office management, dealing with everything from operational systems and procurement to HR administration and software implementation.

That background has given me a useful perspective: technology should solve real business problems, not create new ones.

### Education & Communication

I've taught English to international professionals, including software developers and data engineers.

My work includes:

- Professional English and workplace communication
- Behavioural and technical interview preparation
- Conversational English
- Designing learning resources and interactive activities
- Helping learners build confidence using English in real situations

I've also worked in proofreading and educational content development.

Teaching has influenced how I approach technology. A system might be technically impressive, but if people struggle to understand or use it, something needs improving.
